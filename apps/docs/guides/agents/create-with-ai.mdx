Create with AI is the fastest way to get a first agent. You chat with an assistant about your business, and it fills in a live "Agent sheet" while you talk. This page is for workspace owners and admins who are setting up an agent for the first time (or want a fresh starting point).

<Note>
Only **admins and owners** can use Create with AI. Other roles see a notice instead of the chat.
</Note>

## Before you start

- Know the basics of your business: what you sell or do, who your customers are, and what you want the agent to achieve (answer questions, collect leads, book appointments).
- Have a web page or some text ready if you want the assistant to turn it into knowledge. You can also add knowledge later in [Knowledge](/guides/agents/knowledge-skills).
- On the hosted (SaaS) version, the chat uses AI credits. If your credits run out, the chat stops and shows "AI credits are used up. Buy credits to keep building." See [Usage and credits](/guides/account/usage-and-credits). Self-hosted installs record usage but never block you.

## Create your agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create with AI">
    Go to **Agents** and click **Create with AI**. If you have no agents yet, the same button is on the empty page. The chat opens full screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the assistant's questions">
    The assistant opens with its first question. Type your answer in the box ("Type your answer") and press Enter, or click **Send**. When it offers quick answers, you can click one of the buttons under the chat instead of typing. Use Shift+Enter for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the Agent sheet fill in">
    On the right (or behind the **Agent sheet** bar on a phone) you see six rows fill in as you talk: **Name**, **Persona**, **Greeting**, **Handoff rules**, **Tools** and **Knowledge**. The counter shows how many are covered, for example "4 of 6 covered". A row briefly highlights when it changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add knowledge in the chat (optional)">
    Give the assistant a web page address or paste some text. It can add up to three knowledge sources per chat. It will not accept passwords or secrets, so do not paste any.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the draft">
    When the draft is ready, a card says "Your draft is ready". You can keep chatting to change anything first. Then click **Create draft**, or click **Try it in the sandbox** to create the draft and open the sandbox straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Create draft** needs at least a name, a persona (at least 40 characters) and a greeting. Until those exist the buttons are disabled and the sheet says "Needs a name, a persona and a greeting first."
</Warning>

## What you should see

The dashboard shows "Draft created" with the agent's name and opens the agent editor. The new agent is a **draft**: nothing goes live until you publish it, and customers cannot talk to it yet. Review each tab (Persona, Knowledge, Escalation, Tools), then [test it in the sandbox](/guides/agents/test-in-sandbox) and [publish](/guides/agents/publish-and-versions).

## What the assistant can and cannot set

The assistant writes the same settings you can edit by hand in [Configure your agent](/guides/agents/configure-your-agent):

- Persona (role, scope, tone and rules), greeting, and the fallback, clarifying and handoff messages.
- Escalation keywords, and whether to hand over qualified leads.
- Which built-in tools are on. The chat can switch on **Qualify leads**, **Collect contact details**, **Pass to a person**, **Check calendar availability** and **Book appointments**.
- Knowledge from a web page or pasted text.

It cannot change the AI model, temperature or your own custom tools. Set those in the editor after the draft exists.

<Tip>
Be specific. "We run a dental clinic in Addis Ababa, we want to book cleanings, never give medical advice, and reply in Amharic or English" gives a much better persona than "customer support".
</Tip>

## Limits and common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The chat stopped accepting messages">
    A single chat has a limit of 60 messages from you or about 150,000 tokens of AI work. If you reach it, click **Back to agents**, then start a new chat. Your earlier draft is not lost if you already created it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the assistant is busy or unavailable">
    The AI provider may be temporarily down, or a previous message is still being answered. Wait a moment and send again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I hit a plan limit when creating the draft">
    Your plan allows a set number of agents. Archive an agent you no longer use or upgrade in [Billing and plans](/guides/account/billing-and-plans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I reloaded the page">
    Your chat is remembered for the browser tab you are in, so the conversation and the Agent sheet come back. Closing the tab starts a fresh chat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
