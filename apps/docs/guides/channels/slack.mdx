The Slack channel lets people in your Slack workspace chat with your agent in a direct message. It suits internal helpdesks (IT, HR, office questions) and communities that already live in Slack. This page is for admins and owners, and you need permission to create apps in your Slack workspace.

<Note>
Only **admins and owners** can connect channels. Lumi answers **direct messages** to your Slack app. Messages in public or private channels and group chats are ignored.
</Note>

## Before you start

- A [published agent](/guides/agents/publish-and-versions). Only published agents appear in the **Answering agent** list.
- Permission to create and install a Slack app in your workspace.
- **Self-hosted:** the server's public https address and encryption key must be configured. See [Channels and integrations setup](/self-hosting/channels-and-integrations-setup).

## Connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the Slack app">
    At api.slack.com/apps click **Create New App** and choose to build it from scratch, naming it after your agent and choosing your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add permissions">
    Under **OAuth and permissions**, add the bot scopes `chat:write` (so the app can reply) and `im:history` (so it can read direct messages sent to it). Make sure people can message the app: in **App Home**, allow messages from the Messages tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install it and copy two values">
    Click **Install to Workspace**. Copy the **Bot User OAuth Token** (it starts with `xoxb-`). Then, under **Basic information**, copy the **Signing secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in Lumi">
    In the dashboard go to **Channels** and click **Slack**. Enter a **Connection name**, choose the **Answering agent** (or **No agent**, which means a team member replies), and paste the **Bot token** and **Signing secret**. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Webhook URL">
    The panel says "Shown only once" and shows the **Webhook URL**. Copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Slack where to send messages">
    In your Slack app open **Event subscriptions**, switch them on and paste the Webhook URL as the **Request URL**. Slack checks it immediately and should show "Verified". Under **Subscribe to bot events**, add `message.im`, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In Lumi click **Test connection**; a healthy result reads "Connection OK. Workspace" followed by your workspace name. Click **I have saved these**. Then open a direct message with your Slack app and write to it. The conversation should appear in the [Inbox](/guides/workspace/inbox) and the agent should reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Subscribe to **message.im**. The Slack steps shown in the dashboard also mention `app_mention`, but Lumi currently answers only direct messages, so mentions in channels do not get a reply.
</Warning>

## What people experience

- They find your Slack app under **Apps** and message it like a colleague.
- There is **no reply window**, so you or the agent can answer at any time.
- Messages are text. Files they upload are not read.
- Each person has their own conversation. Lumi creates a contact for them, which you can see in [Contacts](/guides/workspace/contacts).
- When the agent hands off, your team replies from the Lumi [Inbox](/guides/workspace/inbox) and the answer goes back to Slack in the same direct message.

## Manage the connection

Under **Connected**, each Slack connection shows its health (**Connected**, **Needs attention**, **Error**, **Paused**, **Not tested yet**), the last check, masked credentials and Webhook URL.

- **Test connection** runs a live check.
- **Edit** renames it, changes the agent and rotates the bot token or signing secret. Fill in only the field to replace.
- **Disable** / **Enable** pauses it.
- **Delete** keeps conversation history but stops replies.

If you reinstall the Slack app, its bot token changes. Paste the new token under **Edit**.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Slack will not verify the Request URL">
    Check you pasted the full Webhook URL for this connection and that the signing secret in Lumi matches the one on your Slack app's Basic information page. Slack rejects requests that arrive more than five minutes out of date, so the server clock must be correct on self-hosted installs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app does not reply">
    Confirm you subscribed to `message.im`, the app is installed after adding scopes, the connection is not **Paused**, and the agent is published. Also check the conversation was not handed to a person; see [Handoffs](/guides/agents/handoffs-and-human-takeover).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test connection fails">
    The bot token is wrong or the app was uninstalled. Reinstall, copy the new token and use **Edit**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
