Telegram is the easiest messaging channel to set up: you create a free bot, give Lumi its token and tell Telegram where to send messages. This page is for admins and owners. You need a Telegram account and about ten minutes.

<Note>
Only **admins and owners** can connect channels. The bot talks to people in **private chats**. Messages in groups are ignored.
</Note>

## Before you start

- A [published agent](/guides/agents/publish-and-versions). Only published agents appear in the **Answering agent** list.
- A Telegram account on your phone or computer.
- **Self-hosted:** your server must be reachable from the internet over https, and have its public address and encryption key set. See [Channels and integrations setup](/self-hosting/channels-and-integrations-setup).

## Connect Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the bot">
    In Telegram, open the chat with **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`. Choose a name and a username ending in "bot". BotFather replies with a **bot token**, a long text such as `123456:ABC...`. Treat it like a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the dashboard go to **Channels** and click the **Telegram** tile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Enter a **Connection name** (for example "Telegram"), choose the **Answering agent** (or **No agent**, which means a team member replies) and paste the **Bot token**. Leave **Webhook secret token** empty and Lumi generates one. Click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook values">
    The panel says "Shown only once" and shows the **Webhook URL** and the **Webhook secret token**. Copy both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Telegram where to send messages">
    Telegram needs to be told your Webhook URL once. In the same panel click **Set up webhook automatically**. Lumi registers the URL and the secret token with Telegram for you and shows "Webhook registered" when Telegram accepts it. The button is also on the connection card as **Set up webhook**, in case you change the address of your server later. Telegram only accepts `https` addresses, so on a self-hosted server `PUBLIC_API_URL` must be your `https` API address.

    To do it by hand instead, open this address in your browser after replacing the three parts in capitals with your own values, and look for `"ok":true` in the reply:

    `https://api.telegram.org/botYOUR_BOT_TOKEN/setWebhook?url=YOUR_WEBHOOK_URL&secret_token=YOUR_WEBHOOK_SECRET`

    This step is called "setWebhook". The secret token makes sure only Telegram can post messages to your connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    In Lumi click **Test connection**. A healthy result reads "Connection OK. Bot @yourbotname". Click **I have saved these**. Then open your bot in Telegram, press **Start**, and send a message. It should appear in the [Inbox](/guides/workspace/inbox) and the agent should reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Test connection** only checks that Telegram accepts your bot token. It cannot tell you whether the webhook was registered. Always finish by sending a real message to the bot.
</Warning>

## What customers experience

- They find your bot by its username (share the link `t.me/yourbotname` on your website or business card).
- There is **no reply window**: you can answer a conversation at any time, unlike WhatsApp.
- Messages are text. Photos and files from customers arrive as a short text placeholder instead of the file.
- The agent can use all its tools, but forms and buttons turn into plain text, because Telegram messages from Lumi are text-only.

## Manage the connection

Each connection under **Connected** shows its health (**Connected**, **Needs attention**, **Error**, **Paused** or **Not tested yet**), when it was last checked, the masked token and the Webhook URL.

- **Test connection**: a live check.
- **Edit**: rename, change the answering agent or rotate the token or secret. Leave a field empty to keep it.
- **Disable** / **Enable**: pause the bot without deleting it.
- **Delete**: removes the connection. Existing conversations keep their history, but replies can no longer be sent.

If a token was shared by mistake, ask BotFather to revoke it, create a new token and enter it under **Edit**.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The bot does not answer">
    Check that you completed the setWebhook step and saw `"ok":true`. Check the connection is not **Paused**, the agent is published, and on the hosted version that you still have AI credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test connection says it failed">
    The bot token is wrong or was revoked. Copy it again from BotFather and use **Edit**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Messages stopped after I changed the secret">
    If you rotate the **Webhook secret token** in Lumi, run the setWebhook address again with the new secret.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Group messages are ignored">
    This is expected. Only private chats are supported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
