A **tool** is an action your agent can take besides talking: save a customer's details, check your calendar, look up an order. This page is for admins and owners who decide what each agent is allowed to do.

<Note>
Tools are switched on per agent, on the agent's **Tools** tab. Only **admins and owners** can change them. Go to **Agents**, open an agent, then click **Tools**.
</Note>

### Built-in tools

Under **Built-in tools** on the Tools tab you can switch each of these on or off:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Qualify lead** | Captures the customer's need, budget and timeline and moves the conversation to the qualified stage. |
| **Collect contact** | Asks for name, email and phone with a small form, and saves them on the contact. |
| **Escalate to human** | Hands the conversation to your team with a summary. See [Handoffs](/guides/agents/handoffs-and-human-takeover). |
| **Check calendar availability** | Looks up open appointment times on your connected calendar. |
| **Book appointments** | Books a time the customer confirmed. Needs a connected calendar. |

The first three are on by default for new agents. The two calendar tools need a calendar set up first; see [Calendar and booking](/guides/tools/calendar-booking).

### Custom tools

Custom tools let the AI use **your own systems**, for example "where is my order", "is this item in stock", "book a table in our reservation software" or "send this lead to our CRM". You build them without code in a guided builder. See [Build a tool](/guides/tools/build-a-tool). Each tool uses a [connection](/guides/tools/connections), which stores your system's address and login once. An agent can have up to 20 custom tools.

## When does the AI use a tool?

The AI decides during the conversation, based on what the customer says and on the tool's description. You control three things:

1. **Whether it is switched on.** A tool that is off is invisible to the AI.
2. **Which moment of the conversation it may be used in.** The conversation moves through stages: when someone first arrives, while chatting, once qualified, when offering a booking, and after they have booked. Each tool is only offered in its stages. Built-in tools follow fixed rules you can read in the **Stage rules** on the Escalation tab. For custom tools you choose the stages yourself on the last step of the builder.
3. **Whether the customer must confirm.** A custom tool can be set to "Ask the customer to confirm before it runs", which is the right choice for anything that books, orders or changes something.

The AI cannot call a tool that is not allowed in the current stage, even if a customer asks for it. After the conversation is handed to a person (the **handoff requested** stage), no tools run.

## Where to see tool activity

At the top of the Tools tab, **Tool activity** lists the latest times the AI used a custom tool in real conversations, with whether each run worked. It is visible to agents, admins and owners. The sandbox never calls your custom tools or the calendar; see [Test in the sandbox](/guides/agents/test-in-sandbox).

## If a tool fails

For each custom tool you choose what happens when it fails:

- **Hand over to a person**: the conversation goes to your team with a note about what failed (a **System failure** handoff).
- **Tell the customer and carry on**: the AI says it could not do it and keeps helping.

Calendar failures after retries always hand the conversation to a person.

## Changing tools is part of the draft

Switching a tool on or off, or adding a custom tool, changes the agent's draft only. Click **Save draft**, then **Publish** so customers get it. See [Publish and versions](/guides/agents/publish-and-versions).

<Warning>
Custom tools make real requests to your systems when customers use them. Start with read-only tools such as lookups, and use "Ask the customer to confirm before it runs" for anything that changes data.
</Warning>
