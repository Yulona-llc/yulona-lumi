This page is for admins and support leads who decide when the team is available and how handoffs reach people. Business hours tell Yulona when a human can be expected to answer, and the routing settings decide who is asked first.

**Role needed:** anyone can view the settings. Admins and owners can change them. Agents, admins and owners can set their own availability.

## Open the page

Open **Settings** from the left menu, then choose **Hours and routing** (under **Customer chat**). The page has two parts: **Team availability** at the top and **Business hours and routing** below.

## Team availability

Each teammate sets their own status: **online**, **away** or **offline**. Click your status to change it. Below you see a card for every owner, admin and agent with their status and how many conversations they currently have open. The list refreshes every 30 seconds.

When assignment is set to **Least busy online agent**, only people who are **online** are considered for a handoff. Set yourself online when you start work.

## Set your business hours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the timezone">
    Pick your **Timezone** from the list. All windows and holidays use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add weekly windows">
    Under **Weekly windows**, switch a day on to open it. A new day starts as 09:00 to 17:00. Change the start and end times, click **Add window** for a second window the same day (for example a lunch break), or the bin to remove one. **Weekdays 9 to 5** fills Monday to Friday in one click. The **Copy** menu on a day copies its hours **To all days** or **To weekdays**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add holidays">
    Under **Holidays**, pick a date in **Add a date** and add it. Holidays are closed all day and override the weekly windows. Remove one with its cross.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    A bar with **Unsaved changes** appears. Click **Save settings**, or **Discard** to throw the changes away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every window must end after it starts, so a window cannot run past midnight. If you see "Each window must end after it starts", fix the times before saving.

<Note>
With no windows configured, you are treated as always open. Holidays still count as closed.
</Note>

## What happens outside business hours

Under **Outside business hours**, pick one mode:

| Mode | What the AI does |
|---|---|
| **AI only** | The AI keeps answering; nothing special happens. |
| **AI sends a message** | When a conversation needs a person, the AI posts a message saying when you are back. Edit it in **Out-of-hours message** (up to 500 characters); without one, Yulona uses "Our team is currently offline. We have your details and will get back to you as soon as we are open." |
| **Flag in the queue** | Handoffs are marked **Out of hours** in [Needs attention](/guides/workspace/needs-attention). |

Whatever the mode, a handoff created while you are closed is tagged **Out of hours** and the handoff message the customer sees mentions that the team is offline.

## How handoffs are assigned

Under **Handoff assignment**, pick one mode:

| Mode | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Manual claim** | Anyone on the team can claim a handoff. Every owner, admin and agent is notified. |
| **Least busy online agent** | The online teammate with the fewest unresolved conversations gets the handoff and is notified. If nobody is online, it stays unassigned and everyone is notified. |

The suggested teammate still has to claim the item; the conversation stays in **Needs attention** until they do.

## Ticket SLA times

Below the routing settings, **Ticket SLA (minutes to resolve)** sets the deadline for each ticket priority. See [Tickets](/guides/workspace/tickets).

<Note>
Business hours also limit the appointment times the AI offers on Google Calendar. With Calendly, the event type's own availability is used.
</Note>

## What you should see

After saving, new handoffs created outside your hours show an **Out of hours** badge in the queue. A viewer or agent sees the settings but cannot edit them, with a notice that changing them needs a higher role.

## Common problems

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Handoffs never get assigned | Assignment is on **Manual claim**, or nobody is **online**. |
| Everything shows **Out of hours** | Check the **Timezone**. The default is UTC. |
| Settings will not save | A window ends before it starts. |
