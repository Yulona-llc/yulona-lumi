This page is for agents and admins who want to answer faster and keep work organized. **Canned responses** are saved replies you insert in the Inbox. **Tags** are short colored labels.

Both live under **Settings**. Open **Settings** from the left menu, then choose **Canned responses** or **Tags** (under **Customer chat**).

## Canned responses

**Role needed:** anyone can see the list. Agents, admins and owners can create and edit responses. Agents can edit the responses they created; admins and owners can edit any response. Only admins and owners see the delete button.

### Create a canned response

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New response">
    Open **Settings**, **Canned responses**, then click **New response**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Enter a **Shortcut** (lowercase letters, numbers, `-` and `_`, up to 32 characters, and it must start with a letter or number), a **Title** (up to 100 characters) and the **Content** (up to 4,000 characters). The form reminds you the shortcut is "Typed as /shortcut".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Each shortcut must be unique in your workspace; a duplicate is refused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change a response click the pencil next to it. To remove one, an admin clicks the bin and confirms with **Delete**, or **Keep** to cancel.

### Use a canned response in the Inbox

In a conversation, choose **Reply to customer**, then either:

- type `/` followed by part of the shortcut or title. A list of up to six matches appears; click one; or
- click **Canned responses** under the message box and pick from **Insert a saved reply**.

The saved text replaces what is in the message box so you can edit it before sending. The slash list opens only while you have typed a slash and no spaces yet.

<Tip>
Write responses as complete, friendly replies with a placeholder you will fill in by hand, such as "Hi, thanks for reaching out. Your order is on its way, tracking: ". Edit the end before you send.
</Tip>

## Tags

**Role needed:** anyone can see tags. Agents, admins and owners can create them. Only admins and owners can rename or delete them.

### Create a tag

Open **Settings**, **Tags** and click **New tag**. Enter a **Name** (up to 40 characters; names are unique regardless of capitals) and choose a **Color**. Click **Save**. Each tag shows how many items use it.

### Rename or delete a tag

Admins and owners see a pencil and a bin on each tag. Deleting asks "Remove *name* from everything?" and removes the tag from every conversation, contact and ticket it was on.

### Where tags show up

Tags can label conversations, contacts and tickets. Tags attached to a ticket appear next to its status in the ticket's detail panel. Attaching tags to items is currently done through the API (see [Public API](/developers/public-api)); the dashboard lets you manage the tag list itself.

<Note>
Canned responses and tags work the same on the hosted service and on self-hosted installations.
</Note>

## What you should see

After saving a canned response, it appears in the list as its title with a `/shortcut` chip. After saving a tag, it appears as a colored dot and name.

## Common problems

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Typing `/hello` shows nothing | No response matches. Check the shortcut spelling, and that you have not typed a space after the slash text. |
| "Lowercase letters, numbers, - and _ only" | Remove capitals, spaces and other symbols from the shortcut. |
| I cannot delete a response or rename a tag | Those actions need the admin role. |
