The Inbox is where support leads and agents read and answer every conversation from every channel in one place. This guide shows how to find a conversation, tell who said what, reply or take over from the AI, and close the conversation.

**Role needed:** viewers can read the Inbox. Agents, admins and owners can reply, take over, assign and resolve.

## Find a conversation

Open **Inbox** in the left menu. The list on the left shows each conversation with the customer's name (or **Anonymous visitor**), a preview of the last message, how long ago it arrived, a badge and the channel. A small dot marks conversations with a customer message you have not opened yet. Unread dots are remembered per browser.

The badge shows who is answering:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI** | The AI is answering. |
| **Human** | A teammate has taken over. |
| **Needs attention** | The AI stopped and a person should step in. |
| **Resolved** | The conversation is closed. |

A **Live** indicator at the top of the list tells you the connection is up. If it says **Offline** or **Reconnecting**, new messages may be delayed until it recovers.

### Search and filters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search name, email or message**. It matches the contact's name, email and phone, the last message preview, and the text of messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Click the filter button. In **Filter conversations**, pick one option in each group: **Status** (Open, Needs attention, Resolved), **Handled by** (AI, Human), **Channel** (Website chat, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, Slack) and **Assignee** (Anyone, Me, Unassigned, or a teammate). Click **Reset** to clear the filters, or the **Show** button to close the panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear">
    Active filters appear as chips under the search box. Click a chip to remove it, or **Clear all**. Click **Load more** at the bottom of the list for older conversations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `j` | Open the next conversation in the list |
| `k` | Open the previous conversation |
| `/` | Jump to the search box |
| `Ctrl + Enter` (`Cmd + Enter` on Mac) | Send the message you are typing |
| `/` then a shortcut, inside the message box | Insert a [canned response](/guides/workspace/canned-responses-and-tags) |

`j`, `k` and `/` work when you are not typing in a field.

## Read the thread

Each message is labeled with who sent it:

- **Customer** on the left.
- **AI assistant** for AI replies.
- The teammate's name for a human reply.
- The teammate's name followed by **(internal note)** for a note, drawn with a dashed outline. Notes are never sent to the customer.

Centered lines mark events: "*Name* took over", "Handoff requested" with the reason, and "Message not delivered on *channel*" when a reply could not be sent (for example when the 24-hour reply window of a messaging app has closed). A message that failed to send from the dashboard shows **Not sent** with **Retry**. Click **Load earlier messages** to see older history.

The header shows the current state, such as **The AI is answering**, **Waiting for a person**, "*Name* is handling this" or **Resolved**. Next to the contact name you also see the conversation's stage, for example **Qualified**.

The panel on the right (or the **Contact details** button on narrow screens) shows the contact's email, phone and channels, the **Stage**, any handoff reason, the **Lead card** the AI filled in, **Attributes**, and **Earlier conversations**. Click **Open full profile** to go to the [contact](/guides/workspace/contacts).

## Reply, or leave a note

Under the thread, choose **Reply to customer** or **Internal note**, type your message (up to 4,000 characters) and send it.

- A **reply** goes to the customer. If the AI was answering, sending a reply takes over from the AI. If the conversation had no assignee, you become the assignee.
- An **internal note** is visible only to your team. Use it to hand context to a colleague.

Type `/` and the start of a shortcut to insert a canned response, or use the **Canned responses** button.

<Warning>
A resolved conversation cannot be replied to. The box shows "This conversation is resolved. Reopen it to reply." Click **Reopen** first.
</Warning>

## Take over, hand back, assign and resolve

The buttons above the thread control the conversation:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take over** | Stops the AI and assigns the conversation to you. Shown while the AI is answering. |
| **Return to AI** | Hands the conversation back to the AI and unassigns it. This is the label when it is assigned to you. |
| **Hand back to AI** | The same action, when the conversation is handled by someone else. |
| **Assignee** menu | Assign to a teammate or choose **Unassigned**. |
| **Resolve** | Closes the conversation. The AI stops replying. |
| **Reopen** | Opens a resolved conversation again. |

If a teammate already owns the conversation, an agent cannot take it over and sees an error. An admin or owner can.

## What you should see

After **Take over**, the AI badge changes to **Human**, a line "*Your name* took over" appears, and the AI stops answering. After **Return to AI**, the AI answers the next customer message.

## Common problems

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| The AI keeps answering after I replied | Check the badge. If it still says **AI**, click **Take over**. |
| Customer did not receive my WhatsApp reply | Look for a "Message not delivered" line. See [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting). |
| I cannot reply | Your role may be **Viewer**, or the conversation is **Resolved**. |
