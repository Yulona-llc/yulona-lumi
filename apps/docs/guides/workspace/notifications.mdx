This page is for everyone on the team. Notifications tell you when something needs you: a handoff, a ticket assigned to you, a missed deadline, a failed booking, or low credits. They appear in the bell at the top of the dashboard and, for most events, by email.

**Role needed:** every role has notifications. What you receive depends on your role and on what is assigned to you.

## The notification bell

The bell sits at the top right of every page. A badge shows how many notifications you have not read (it shows 99+ when there are more). The list refreshes by itself about every 30 seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bell">
    Click the bell. You see **Notifications** with your latest items, unread ones highlighted with a dot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click a notification. It is marked as read and takes you to the right place: handoffs open **Needs attention**, tickets open **Tickets**, bookings open **Bookings**, and items tied to a conversation open it in the **Inbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark everything read">
    Click **Mark all read** to clear the badge. When nothing is left, the list says "You are all caught up."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you are notified about

| Event | Who is notified | Also emailed |
|---|---|---|
| A handoff is requested | Every active owner, admin and agent (when assignment is **Manual claim**, or nobody is online) | Yes |
| A handoff is assigned to you | The teammate chosen by **Least busy online agent**, or assigned by an admin | Yes |
| A ticket is assigned to you | The new assignee | Yes |
| A ticket misses its SLA deadline | The assignee, or every owner and admin when unassigned | Yes |
| A booking is created | The teammate who created it, or every owner and admin when the AI booked | No |
| A booking fails | Every owner and admin | Yes |
| AI credits reach 80% | Every owner and admin (hosted service) | Yes |
| AI credits are used up | Every owner and admin (hosted service) | Yes |

The credit warnings are sent once each per month. See [Usage and credits](/guides/account/usage-and-credits).

<Note>
The two credit notifications only exist on the hosted service. Self-hosted installations do not meter credits, so you never receive them.
</Note>

## Email notifications

Emails are sent shortly after the notification is created, to the address you sign in with. Lumi retries a few times if sending fails. Every account has email notifications switched on by default.

<Warning>
Email delivery depends on the email provider configured for your installation. On the hosted service this is managed for you. On a self-hosted installation, if no email provider is configured, notifications still appear in the bell but emails are not delivered. Ask your administrator.
</Warning>

## Common problems

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| I get no handoff notifications | Viewers do not receive them. With **Least busy online agent**, only the assigned person is notified, so set your status to **online** under **Settings**, **Hours and routing**. |
| A notification opens nothing | Credit and some system notices have no page to open; read the text in the list. |
| No emails | Check spam, then ask your administrator whether email sending is set up. |
| Too many handoff emails | Assign handoffs with **Least busy online agent** so only one person is notified each time. See [Business hours](/guides/workspace/business-hours). |
