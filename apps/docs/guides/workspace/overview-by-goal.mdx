The Overview is your home page. It shows the numbers that matter for the goals you chose, plus what needs a person right now. This page is for everyone in the workspace; owners and admins can also change the goals.

## Choose your goals

Goals are chosen in the [setup wizard](/guides/workspace/setup-wizard) or on the Overview itself. If no goal is set, the Overview shows **Tell Lumi your goal** with buttons for **Answer questions**, **Capture leads** and **Take bookings**. Viewers and agents see "Ask a workspace admin to choose the goal." You can also say it to [Ask Lumi](/guides/workspace/ask-lumi).

If you pick several goals, all their blocks show, with your first goal on top. All numbers cover the last 30 days.

## Customer questions (goal: answer questions)

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Answered by Lumi | Share of chats where a customer wrote and Lumi never needed to hand over to a person. |
| Handed to your team | How many handovers happened. |
| First reply | The typical time (median) until the first answer, in seconds. |
| Questions Lumi could not answer | Handovers because Lumi did not know. Click one to open the chat, and use **Add knowledge** to fill the gap. |

Until there is data the cards say "No data yet" instead of zero.

## Leads (goal: capture leads)

- **Funnel**: Started, Engaged, Qualified, Booking offered and Booked, counted per conversation.
- **Qualified leads**: conversations that reached Qualified.
- **HubSpot**: whether it is connected, when it last synced and how many records failed. If it is not connected, a link takes you to **Connect HubSpot**. See [HubSpot](/guides/tools/hubspot).

## Bookings (goal: take bookings)

**Upcoming**, **Booked** and **Cancelled** (the last two cover the last 30 days), plus whether a calendar is connected. If none is, a link opens **Connect a calendar**. See [Calendar and booking](/guides/tools/calendar-booking).

## On every Overview

- **Set up your workspace** checklist (owners and admins) until the steps are done, with **Try it, then publish** after the setup wizard.
- AI credits, **Right now** (how many chats the AI is handling and who is waiting), **Needs you** and the activity charts.
