**Tickets** are for support leads and agents who need to follow an issue beyond a single chat. Each ticket has a number, a status, a priority, an owner and a deadline (the SLA), and keeps a complete activity history.

**Role needed:** anyone can view tickets. Agents, admins and owners can create and change them. Only admins and owners can delete them.

## Open the Tickets page

Open **Tickets** in the left menu, under **Operations**. A switch at the top right lets you choose **Board** or **Table**. On a phone the board appears as **List**, with one status at a time.

### Board

The board has five columns: **Open**, **In progress**, **Waiting**, **Resolved** and **Closed**. The number on each column is the total of tickets in it, and each column shows the 50 most recently updated. To change a ticket's status, drag its card to another column on a computer, or use the **Move to** menu on the card (this also works on touch screens and with the keyboard). Click a card to open the ticket.

### Table

The table shows **Ticket** (number and title), **Status**, **Priority**, **SLA**, **Assignee** and **Updated**. Click a title to open the ticket and **Load more** for older tickets.

## Create a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New ticket">
    On the Tickets page click **New ticket**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Enter a **Title** (required, up to 200 characters), an optional **Description**, a **Priority** (Low, Normal, High or Urgent; Normal by default) and an optional **Assignee**. Click **Create ticket**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The ticket gets the next number for your workspace, such as #1042. An assignee is notified.

## Search and filter

Type in **Search title, description or #number** to search. Use **Priority** and **Assignee** (Anyone, Me, Unassigned, or a teammate) to narrow the list. In the table view you can also filter by **Status**. Switch on **SLA breached only** to see tickets that missed their deadline.

## SLA and priorities

Every ticket gets a deadline when it is created, based on its priority:

| Priority | Default time to resolve |
|---|---|
| Urgent | 1 hour |
| High | 4 hours |
| Normal | 24 hours |
| Low | 3 days |

Admins can change these under **Settings**, **Hours and routing**, in **Ticket SLA (minutes to resolve)**. See [Business hours](/guides/workspace/business-hours). Changing a ticket's priority recalculates the deadline from the moment it was created.

A badge shows the state of the timer: green when on track, amber when due within the hour, and **SLA breached** in red once the deadline passes. Resolved and closed tickets stop counting. When a ticket breaches, the assignee is notified. If it has no assignee, every owner and admin is notified.

## Work a ticket

Click a ticket to open the detail panel.

- Change **Status**, **Priority** and **Assignee** with the menus at the top. Each change is saved right away and recorded.
- Read **SLA due** and **Created**, the contact, and the **Open conversation** link when the ticket is tied to a chat.
- Type in **Add a comment** and click **Comment** to add a note for your team.
- The **Timeline** lists every change in order: created, status and priority changes, assignments, comments and SLA breaches, with who did it and when.
- Admins and owners see **Delete ticket**, which removes the ticket and its history permanently.

## Bulk actions

In the table, tick the boxes next to several tickets (or the box in the header to select all). A bar appears with **Status**, **Priority**, **Assign** and, for admins and owners, **Delete**. Pick a value and it applies to every selected ticket. A message tells you how many were updated and how many were skipped. Click the cross to clear the selection. You can act on up to 100 tickets at once.

<Warning>
Deleting tickets removes them and their history for good.
</Warning>

## What you should see

New tickets appear in **Open** with a priority badge and a green SLA badge. When you move one to **Resolved**, its SLA badge disappears.

## Common problems

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| No **New ticket** button | Your role is **Viewer**. |
| A ticket shows **SLA breached** | The deadline passed while it was open. Resolve it, or ask an admin to adjust the SLA times. Reopening a ticket in time clears the breach. |
| Cards will not drag | Use the **Move to** menu on the card. |
