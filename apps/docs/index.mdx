Yulona gives your business an AI agent that answers customers on your website and messaging apps, asks the right questions to qualify leads, books appointments, and hands the conversation to a person when it should. This page is for business owners, support leads and agents deciding where to start.

## What you can do with Yulona

- **Answer customers around the clock.** The agent replies from the knowledge you give it: help pages, PDFs, pasted text. If it cannot find an answer, it says so and passes the conversation to your team instead of guessing.
- **Turn visitors into leads.** The agent moves each conversation through fixed stages (visitor, engaged, qualified, booking offered, booked) and captures what the customer needs in a lead card.
- **Book appointments.** Connect Google Calendar or Calendly and the agent offers times and books them.
- **Work every channel from one inbox.** Website chat, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram and Slack conversations all land in the same Inbox, with a single timeline for each contact.
- **Step in whenever you want.** Take over a conversation, leave an internal note, or hand it back to the AI. The Needs attention queue shows every conversation where the AI stopped and a person should help.
- **Track the follow-up.** Tickets with SLA timers, bookings, and transactions sit next to the conversations that created them.

<Note>
Yulona runs in two ways. On the hosted service, Yulona provides the AI and meters it in credits. On a self-hosted installation, your own organization runs Yulona with its own AI provider account, and credits are never charged. Where a page behaves differently, a note says so.
</Note>
